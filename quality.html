Testing Equipment

In-House QA Testing

Every batch is tested before dispatch using industry-standard equipment ensuring zero compromise on quality.

Quality Lab

Dedicated in-house QA laboratory with industry standard testing instruments for all incoming and outgoing material checks.

Bursting Strength Tester

Evaluates the pressure resistance of corrugated board before rupture, safeguarding product integrity during stacking and transit.

Burst Factor Tester

Validates burst factor ratings on kraft liners and corrugated boards against customer specified minimum requirements.

Crush Tester (CCT/ECT)

Evaluates the resistance of corrugated fluting to crushing forces, ensuring board stiffness and structural integrity under load (also performs Ring Crush, Edge Crush, and Flat Crush tests).

Edge Cutter

Produces uniform samples for edge crush tests, ensuring precise assessment of carton strength.

Box Compression Tester (BCT)

Tests the maximum compressive load a finished box can bear ensures safe stacking in warehouses and during transit.

Flat Crush Tester (FCT)

Evaluates the resistance of corrugated flute to flat crushing — a key indicator of fluting medium quality and corrugation integrity.

Puncture Resistance Tester

Measures the energy needed to puncture the corrugated board critical for fragile goods and sharp edged contents.

COBB Tester

Measures the water absorption of the board surface. Ensures moisture resistance for cold chain and outdoor storage applications.

Ring Cutter

Cuts precise ring samples from paper for Ring Crush Test — evaluates the compressive strength of the liner and medium.

Caliper Gauge

Precisely measures the thickness (caliper) of the corrugated board ensures compliance with customer specified ply specifications.

GSM Balance

Weighs paper samples to determine Grams per Square Metre (GSM) verifies raw material grammage against order specs.

Moisture Meter

Checks moisture content of kraft paper before production to prevent delamination and weak corrugation bonds.

Moisture Analyser

Precision moisture analysis of board samples at different production stages, ensuring consistent glue bond and structural stability.

Viscosity Meter

Monitors the viscosity of starch adhesive during corrugation, ensuring optimal bonding strength throughout the production run.

Policies & Compliance

Our Governance Framework

Quality Policy

  • Deliver defect free products First Time Right, Every Time
  • High degree of customer orientation with meeting all quality standards
  • Meet or exceed customer specifications on every order
  • Continual improvement of QMS processes and KPIs
  • Employee training on quality standards and procedures
  • Supplier evaluation and material quality audits

Environmental Policy

  • Use of water based, non toxic flexographic inks only
  • FSC compliant kraft liners from certified suppliers
  • Zero waste to landfill plant policy all scrap recycled
  • Energy efficient machinery and LED plant lighting
  • Implementing water-saving best practices in line with Coca-Cola's guidance to strengthen sustainability across our operations

Health & Safety Policy

  • Mandatory PPE safety shoes, aprons
  • Regular machine guarding and safety audits
  • Fire safety compliance with extinguishers and drills
  • Ergonomic workstation design for operator welfare
  • Zero tolerance for unsafe working conditions

HR & Labour Policy

  • Fair wages compliant as per Maharashtra state Minimum Wage Act
  • Provident Fund (PF) and ESIC for all employees
  • No child labour
  • Committed to equal opportunity
  • Annual performance reviews and skill development training

Company Profile

Pune's Most Trusted Corrugated Box Manufacturer

At Shivam Cartons Pvt. Ltd. we believe packaging is more than protection, it is a promise of quality, reliability and innovation. As a trusted partner to leading industries across India, we specialize in manufacturing high-performance corrugated cartons that meet the most demanding standards of strength, sustainability and precision. Established in 1999 under the leadership of Mr. Sachin Gore, with extensive experience in corrugated packaging, Shivam Cartons Pvt. Ltd. has consistently delivered excellence in packaging solutions. We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and proud member of FCBM & WICMA.

Mission & Values

What Drives Us Every Day

Our Mission

Deliver cartons that don’t just carry products they carry trust, safety and value.

Our Vision

To be the preferred packaging partner for businesses worldwide by combining innovation, sustainability and operational excellence.

Sustainability

100% recyclable kraft liners, water-based inks, FSC-compliant materials and green packaging by design.

Customer First

Our dedicated teams, swift sampling processes and adaptable MOQs ensure that every client engagement is treated as a strategic partnership rather than a simple transaction.

Quality Assurance

Rigorous in-house QA — Burst Factor, ECT, COBB, GSM, Moisture Test, etc. At every stage of production we ensure zero compromise on quality.

In-House Production

Complete end to end manufacturing under one roof from raw kraft paper to finished products, bundled cartons ready for dispatch.

1999

Foundation

Shivam Cartons Pvt. Ltd. founded by Mr. Sachin Gore with a vision to provide quality corrugated packaging to Pune's growing industries.

2005

Plant Expansion

Expanded and relocated to Chandkhed, Maval facility — a 40,000 sq. ft. state of the art manufacturing plant.

2008

Through ISO 9001 certification, we continuously strengthen our commitment to quality management and customer satisfaction as per ISO standards.

Advanced Technologies
